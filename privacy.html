Privacy Policy

Scribewell for Mac · Last updated November 10, 2026

What Scribewell collects

Nothing. Scribewell does not collect, upload, sell, or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising code, and no tracking. The app has no network access of its own.

Your recordings and transcripts

Scribewell reads only the audio and video files you choose. Speech is turned into text by macOS on your Mac (on-device recognition): your recordings are not sent to Scribewell, to Apple, or to anyone else. Transcripts and settings are saved only on your Mac, inside Scribewell's private storage. Deleting a transcript or the app removes them. Your original files are never changed.

Speech models

If your Mac doesn't yet have the speech model for a language, macOS may download it from Apple. That download is made by macOS, contains no audio or personal data, and is the same model macOS uses for dictation.

Purchases

Scribewell Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Scribewell only learns whether Pro is unlocked, never your name or payment details.

Children

Scribewell does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
